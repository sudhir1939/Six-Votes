# Reading

Read all three before you start. Budget about 30 minutes.

1. Is the #1 AI Model Really #1?
   https://towardsdatascience.com/is-the-1-ai-model-really-1/
2. Your Six AI Models Are Really Two: The Math of Correlated Mistakes
   https://towardsdatascience.com/your-six-ai-models-are-really-two-the-math-of-correlated-mistakes/
3. A Neural Network Learned to Read Digits From Pure Noise
   https://towardsdatascience.com/a-neural-network-learned-to-read-digits-from-pure-noise/

While reading, note for each article: the main idea, the method it uses, and one caveat.
You will need these for the one-page write-up, where you say what you took from each
article and where you used it in the project.
